Our Sweet Story

Baking with passion since 2015

Welcome to CAKLORA

Founded by pastry chef Clara, CAKLORA started as a small home bakery with a big dream: to spread joy through delicious, beautiful baked goods. Our name combines "cake" and "flora" – representing our belief that every dessert should be as delightful as a blooming flower.

Today, we continue to bake with the same love and attention to detail, using only the finest ingredients to create unforgettable treats for every occasion. From custom wedding cakes to daily fresh pastries, every item is made with care and creativity.

Visit our cozy bakery and experience the magic of freshly baked happiness!
